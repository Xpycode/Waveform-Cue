# Appearance panes acceptance on the M4 Pro

On November 28, 2026 the integrated Debug suite passed all 339 tests on macOS 15.8.1 with zero skips. It included a real AVC-Intra MXF encode and decode. The on-screen evidence comes from Waveform Cue in the hidden Atlas login with synthetic audio and disposable queues. The Wave 4 helper ran the signed 0.1 (17) development build from Wave 3. Its app and package sources matched P4.1. [Integration measurements](integration.md) · [Specification](../../../specs/appearance-panes-picture-layout.md)

The row numbers follow the specification in order. **Verified** identifies the behavior established by the cited evidence on this Mac. **Not verified** identifies the specific missing observation. **External** identifies an operator, VoiceOver or second-Mac check in the plan.
